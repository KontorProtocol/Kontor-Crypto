- Nova/Arecibo’s `RecursiveSNARK::prove_step` has a deliberate early-return quirk. The VERY FIRST call to `prove_step` after `new()` is a *NO-OP* — it bumps the internal step counter but DOES NOT SYNTHESIZE THE CIRCUIT. This is by design. Therefore, if you want to produce a proof with N steps (N challenges), you MUST: 1. Call `RecursiveSNARK::new(...)` once (this executes step 0 with synthesis). 2. Call `prove_step(...)` EXACTLY N times (not N−1). The first call is a no-op, and only calls 2..N actually synthesize steps 1..N−1. 3. Verify with `num_steps = N`.
